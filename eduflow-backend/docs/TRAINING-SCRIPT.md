# 30-Minute Administrative Staff Training Script

**Audience:** College Administrators, HODs, IQAC Coordinators, Non-Teaching Staff  
**Platform:** EduFlow AI OS (Administrative Automation & AI Officers)  
**Total Duration:** 30 Minutes

---

## 00:00 – 05:00: Platform Overview & Login
- **Presenter Action:** Open the portal on the presentation display.
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Welcome everyone. EduFlow AI is not another complex ERP where you manually enter data for hours. It is an intelligent administrative copilot designed to eliminate repetitive paperwork."*
  - *"Each department has an HOD account, and administrative coordinators have designated staff access."*
  - Demonstrate login using institutional email and strong 10-character password.
  - Highlight the clean dashboard overview, live student metrics, and the persistent Top Navigation bar with Global Search and Notification Center.

---

## 05:00 – 10:00: The 5 AI Officers & Observation Mode
- **Presenter Action:** Open `/demo/observe` and trigger the cinematic observation sequence.
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Notice the 5 specialized AI Officers operating simultaneously: Accreditation, Timetable, Admissions, Finance, and Student Success."*
  - Point to the live token streaming: *"The Accreditation Officer is auditing Criteria 1 through 7, cross-referencing your curriculum against 2024 NAAC RAF standards."*
  - Point to the ROI tracker: *"Within 2 minutes, this saves over 35 hours of manual committee paperwork, representing over ₹50,000 in administrative value."*

---

## 10:00 – 15:00: Academic Calendar & Attendance Ingestion
- **Presenter Action:** Navigate to Academic Calendar and Attendance modules.
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Here is our Academic Calendar 2026. With one click, it syncs national holidays and our state festival breaks."*
  - Demonstrate interactive vacation adjustment: *"If the university extends Dussehra break by 3 days, watch how the exam timetable automatically reschedules forward."*
  - Move to Attendance Integration: *"Click 'Try with Sample Data' to see how the portal imports student logs, automatically detects attendance shortages below 75%, and triggers early alerts before end-semesters."*

---

## 15:00 – 20:00: Timetable Scheduling & Conflict-Free Deltas
- **Presenter Action:** Open Timetable Upload page.
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Uploading last semester's Excel sheet allows us to apply modifications without starting from scratch."*
  - Show the 4 delta rules: elective introductions, sabbatical faculty replacements, and 3-hour lab block consolidations.
  - *"The engine regenerates a conflict-free grid in seconds, with complete color-coded before-and-after audit logs."*

---

## 20:00 – 25:00: Staff Delegation & HOD Approvals
- **Presenter Action:** Open HOD Staff Management and switch to Staff Dashboard.
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"HODs maintain total control while delegating tasks. For example, our Academic Coordinator Priya can prepare timetable drafts and view accreditation reports, but cannot publish without HOD approval."*
  - Submit a timetable change as Priya $\rightarrow$ show the pending draft appearing in the HOD dashboard $\rightarrow$ click 'Approve'.
  - Show the real-time notification appearing instantly on Priya's screen.

---

## 25:00 – 30:00: Invoicing, UPI Collections & Q&A
- **Presenter Action:** Open Invoice Generator and UPI Collection page.
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Invoices are auto-numbered with GST compliance. Each invoice generates a pre-filled UPI link and QR code for direct bank remittance without gateway fees."*
  - Show the 'Download My Data' full backup button in Settings.
  - Point to the Help Center (`/help`) and interactive Documentation (`/docs`) in the top navbar.
  - Open floor for questions.
