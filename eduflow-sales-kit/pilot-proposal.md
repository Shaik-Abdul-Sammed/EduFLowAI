## 1. Executive Summary
EduFlow AI is an autonomous institutional intelligence and accreditation operating system purpose-built for Indian higher educational institutions. Utilizing domain-tuned Autonomous AI Officers (Accreditation, Student Success, Timetable, Admissions, and Finance), EduFlow AI ingests transactional ERP/LMS data, validates compliance frameworks, and autonomously drafts statutory accreditation documents (NAAC SSR, NIRF, NBA) with verifiable mathematical accuracy.

This Agreement formalizes a 30-Day, Zero-Cost Evaluation Pilot enabling the Institution's Leadership and Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) to experience automated SSR generation with zero financial commitment and zero operational disruption.

---

## 2. Scope of the Evaluation Pilot
During the 30-day evaluation period, EduFlow AI shall deploy its Autonomous AI Officer suite across **one (1) designated academic department** (e.g., Department of Computer Science & Engineering or Information Technology).

### Deliverables:
1. **Automated SSR Criterion Generation:** Complete generation of **NAAC Criterion 2: Teaching-Learning & Evaluation** (Metrics 2.1 through 2.7) formatted to NAAC Revised Accreditation Framework (RAF 2024) specifications.
2. **Predictive Student Retention Assessment:** Real-time flagging of at-risk academic dropouts and fee defaulters within the pilot department.
3. **Executive Intelligence Dashboard:** Real-time Dean/Principal KPI portal displaying faculty cadre ratios, student retention forecasts, and curriculum completion indices.

## 4. Data Privacy, Sovereignty & Compliance (DPDP Act 2023)
The Provider recognizes that academic and institutional records are confidential sovereign property of the Institution.

1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Conformance:** All student and faculty Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3.
2. **Zero Data Monetization & Zero LLM Training:** Institutional data is strictly segregated in dedicated tenant namespaces. Data is **never** used to train public language models, shared with third parties, or monetized.
3. **Local Indian Cloud Residency:** Database clusters reside within compliant Indian cloud regions (MeitY-empanelled / Mumbai/Hyderabad data centers).
4. **Data Purge Guarantee:** Upon termination of the pilot without contract extension, all stored institutional data is completely purged within 7 business days upon written confirmation.
