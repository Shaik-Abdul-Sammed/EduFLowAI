# Getting Started with EduFlow AI OS

Welcome to EduFlow AI OS! Follow this 5-step quickstart:

1. **Complete Onboarding:** Go to `/admin-dashboard/onboarding` and enter your institution details.
2. **Import Departments & Faculty:** Upload your rosters via standard CSV templates.
3. **Sync Academic Calendar:** Generate your state-specific 2026 academic calendar.
4. **Delegate Staff Permissions:** Assign coordinator roles with granular approval safeguards.
5. **Launch AI Officers:** Generate NAAC SSR criteria audits and conflict-free timetables in real-time.
