# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Does EduFlow replace our existing ERP?
No. EduFlow seamlessly integrates on top of your existing SIS/ERP or runs standalone with sample CSV data.

### 2. Can non-teaching staff publish timetables directly?
No. Staff members submit drafts that require explicit HOD review and approval.

### 3. How are payments processed without a gateway fee?
EduFlow supports direct institutional UPI links and QR codes directly crediting your college bank account.
