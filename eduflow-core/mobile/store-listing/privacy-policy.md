# EduFlow AI Mobile App Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Governing Law:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023, India) & Information Technology Act, 2000

---

## 1. Introduction
EduFlow AI ("EduFlow", "we", "us", or "our") provides the EduFlow AI Mobile Application to students, faculty, and administrators of affiliated educational institutions ("Institutions"). This Privacy Policy describes how we process, store, and safeguard your personal data.

The educational institution that enrolled you or employs you acts as the **Data Fiduciary**, and EduFlow AI acts as a **Data Processor** operating strictly on the instruction of the Institution.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect
We collect only the minimum personal data necessary to provide institutional educational services:
- **Identification & Account Data:** Full Name, Institutional Email Address, Roll Number / Employee ID, Phone Number, Assigned Role (Student, Faculty, Staff, Administrator).
- **Academic & Attendance Data:** Class attendance timestamps, course enrollments, examination grades, and academic timetable assignments.
- **Financial Transaction Data:** Invoice reference IDs, fee amounts paid, and Bank/UPI UTR numbers (EduFlow does not store payment card numbers or banking PINs).
- **Device & Technical Data:** IP address, device model, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens for receiving institutional emergency notifications.

---

## 3. Purpose of Processing
Your data is processed strictly for legitimate educational uses:
- Recording course attendance and generating statutory NAAC/AICTE compliance reports.
- Delivering class timetable schedules and real-time room change alerts.
- Facilitating student tuition fee tracking and instant digital receipt issuance.
- Notifying guardians and students of academic alerts or risk indicators.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Localization
In accordance with Indian Data Protection regulations, **all personal data is stored and processed exclusively within data centers located in the Republic of India (Mumbai Region / AWS ap-south-1)**. We do not transfer personal data outside the territory of India without explicit governmental authorization.

---

## 5. Security & Encryption
- **In Transit:** All communications between the mobile application and backend APIs are encrypted using TLS 1.3 with forward secrecy.
- **At Rest:** Database records and backups are encrypted using AES-256 (TDE).
- **Database Isolation:** PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) guarantees complete tenant isolation between different universities.

---

## 6. Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act 2023)
As a Data Principal, you have the following statutory rights under the DPDP Act 2023:
1. **Right to Access Information:** View the personal data held about you in the app profile.
2. **Right to Correction & Erasure:** Request rectification of inaccurate academic data or deletion upon graduation/withdrawal (subject to institutional academic recordkeeping mandates).
3. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** Contact our appointed Data Protection Officer.
