# EduFlow AI OS — Strategic Business Model & Unit Economics

## 1. Growth Horizons

### Phase 1: Automation Service Business (Current)
- Selling bespoke NAAC/NBA accreditation report packages directly to educational institutions.
- Package pricing: ₹49,000 (Pilot Audit) to ₹1,99,000 (Full SSR Suite).
- Delivery model: Secure tokenized URL links (`/r/:token`) and GST-compliant electronic invoices.

### Phase 2: Productized SaaS
- Recurring annual subscriptions per institution (₹3.5L – ₹12L/year).
- Direct self-service access to the 5 AI Officers, continuous IQAC tracking, and timetable optimization.

---

## 2. Unit Economics
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) per Report:**
  - LLM API inference tokens: ~₹150 – ₹300 per comprehensive 7-criteria run.
  - Server compute & bandwidth: ~₹25.
  - Total COGS: < ₹350 per deliverable report.
- **Gross Margin:** > 98% gross margin on report generation services.
